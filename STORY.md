# SNITCH — the game script (v1, 2026-11-19)

> This project is not audited, endorsed or operated by Zama. Use at your own risk.

## The one-line pitch
Ten years ago someone sold out your partner, Ash, and he died in a police raid. Now you are back in
Port Solace building a new crew, and the same voice is on your phone again: *"One of them is mine."*

## Why people keep playing
1. **A mystery that runs across the whole game**, on top of the snitch in each chapter. Who sold Ash out? Every
   chapter ends on a cliffhanger, and every 3-star job unlocks one **evidence file** that moves the real
   story forward. Collect all 24 files to unlock the true ending.
2. **Every job is a short mission, not a coin toss.** You scout, plan, make choices during the robbery,
   escape, and then read what happened afterwards (see "How one job plays").
3. **Mistakes stay with you.** A crew member who gets arrested is gone for the chapter. A wrong
   accusation makes the others trust you less. Heat carries from job to job. You can't win by luck.
4. **The snitch is drawn fresh, in secret, every time you play.** Replaying a chapter is a new puzzle,
   not a rerun.

## The cast
| Who | What they are | Their story |
|---|---|---|
| **You, the Boss** | Your chosen look (the Don, the Widow, the Kid, the Ghost) | You walked away from the night Ash died. You never found out who talked. |
| **Ash** | Your old partner | "Died" in the Harbour raid ten years ago. Nobody ever found the body. |
| **Detective Inspector Hale** | The cop who runs the informants | Ash's younger sister. She thinks *you* sold Ash out, and she has waited ten years. |
| **Echo** | A voice on a burner phone | Texts you after every job. Knows things only the crew knows. |
| **Juno** | The fence who buys your stolen goods | Old friend. Was there the night Ash died. Joins the crew in chapter 2. |
| **Vex, Mara, Dutch, Pixel, Sable, Rook** | The crew | Each has a skill (driver, safecracker, muscle, hacker, lookout, inside man) and a small story of their own. |

**Main rule:** in every chapter, the game secretly picks who the snitch is, sealed and encrypted. No one
(not even us) knows who it is until the run ends. The *story* is written; the *snitch* never is.

## How one job plays (5 steps, about 3–5 minutes)
Today a job is "pick a team, watch a film" (30–60 seconds). Each job becomes five steps:

1. **Scout**: pick one move before the job:
   - **Case the place:** see the job's twist in advance (needs X, X stays home, rush, alarm…).
   - **Buy the blueprints:** costs cash, and makes the inside task easier.
   - **Pay off a beat cop:** costs cash, and makes plain bad luck less likely on this job (the snitch can still tip the police).
2. **Plan**: pick the team **and give each person a role** (driver, inside, lookout, safe). The right
   skill in the right role raises the take; the wrong one adds risk. The snitch's skill is real too,
   so leaving your best safecracker at home has a cost.
3. **The job**: the robbery plays as a film, and you make **2–3 quick decisions** along the way:
   - *"A patrol car turns onto the street." Wait (lose time) or go now (risk)?*
   - *"The clerk reached under the counter." Grab what's there and run (half the take) or finish the safe?*
   - **A short hands-on task** that matches what you're stealing: turn the safe dial, cut the right alarm wire,
     pick which case to smash, work out the vault code from what the lookout heard.
   Your choices change the take and the risk. **Whether the snitch called the police is already decided in secret** and
   is only revealed when the police do or don't come through the door. That keeps the moment of suspense.
4. **Getaway**: if the police were tipped, choose the escape route (alleys, the docks, the tunnel). A bad
   pick means someone gets **lifted** (out for the next job) on top of the heat.
5. **Aftermath**: split the cash, watch the crew react, and read **what the street is saying**: one new rumour
   about one crew member. Rumours are often right but not always (worked out in secret, like tails). Then Echo texts.

**Between jobs (the story part):** a phone call from Hale, a text from Echo, a crew member who wants to talk
alone, a choice ("Juno wants a bigger cut. Pay her or let her walk?"). Some of these give you tools; some
cost you trust.

## The chapters (prologue + 8 chapters, 60 jobs, about 4–5 hours for a first playthrough)

Each chapter: a **story opening**, 6–8 jobs, a **boss job** at the end, then a **cliffhanger**. You need at least
one star to open the next chapter, and 3 stars on a job gives you an evidence file.

### Prologue: The Harbour (3 jobs, tutorial, ~12 min)
*Ten years ago. You, Ash and three others. Someone already talked.*
- **Teaches:** pick a team, take part in the job, read the aftermath, use your one tail, accuse.
- Jobs: Corner store → Bookmaker → **The Harbour (boss)**: the raid. Whatever you do, Ash goes down.
  You see someone's hand on a phone and never see the face.
- **Cliffhanger:** *"Ten years later."* Your phone buzzes: **"Welcome back. One of them is mine. — Echo"**

### 1. The Corner (6 jobs)
*Small jobs to rebuild. Five faces. One is wearing a wire.*
- New: **roles and skills**, **scouting**, **rumours**.
- Jobs: Pawn shop · Card room · Bookie's office (safe) · Jeweller (rush: 25 s) · Gold exchange ·
  **Night deposit (boss)**: four people, need Mara on the safe.
- Story: Hale visits your bar "for a drink". Echo texts the take of your last job *exactly*.
- **Cliffhanger:** at the end you find a photo of Ash, dated *last month*.

### 2. Uptown (7 jobs)
*Bigger doors. A bigger crew. And a wiretap of your own.*
- New: **the wiretap** (pick two people; it tells you for certain if the snitch is one of them, once per chapter). **Juno joins.**
- Jobs: Gallery · Penthouse (dark: no tails that night) · Wine cellar · Auction house (hot: double take,
  double heat) · Tailor's back room · Private club · **Private bank vault (boss)**
- Story: Juno says she saw Ash's car outside the old harbour. Hale moves into the flat across the street.
- **Cliffhanger:** the wiretap picks up a voice saying your real name. Nobody in this city should know it.

### 3. The Docks (7 jobs)
*Someone is talking. Someone else is stealing.*
- New: **the skimmer** (a second hidden traitor who takes half the cash on clean jobs).
- Jobs: Customs shed (rush) · Container nine · Fuel depot · Harbour office (dark) · Fish market cold store ·
  Crane yard · **The freighter (boss, hot)**
- Story: you go back to the place where Ash died. Pixel finds a deleted police file: the Harbour raid informant had a code name, **ECHO**.
- **Cliffhanger:** Echo texts a photo of *you*, taken tonight, from inside the hideout.

### 4. Two Wires (7 jobs)
*Hale turned two of them.*
- New: **two snitches**. Tails and rumours get muddier, and catching one doubles the bag only once.
- Jobs: Armoured van (on the street) · Casino cage · Evidence locker (rob the police) · Bus depot payroll ·
  Racetrack bookies · Diamond courier · **Casino vault (boss)**
- Story: Hale offers *you* a deal: give her Ash's killer and she lets you walk. You realise she means you.
- **Cliffhanger:** one of your crew is found beaten in an alley with "ECHO" written on the wall.

### 5. The Turn (7 jobs)
*The snitch is recruiting.*
- New: **the snitch turns others.** If you have not caught the snitch by job 4, a second crew member
  turns, in secret. Speed matters now.
- Jobs: Pharmacy warehouse · Jewellery fair · Art restorer · Ferry ticket office · Gold refinery ·
  Mayor's fundraiser · **City treasury van (boss)**
- Story: Juno admits she was at the harbour that night, then disappears.
- **Cliffhanger:** Ash walks into your bar, alive. *"We need to talk about who sold me."*

### 6. Blood Money (7 jobs)
*The crew starts asking questions about you.*
- New: **loyalty**. Wrong accusations and unfair splits lower it. Below half, members refuse jobs or
  walk out. The **sit-down vote**: before the boss job the crew can vote someone out, sometimes you're wrong.
- Jobs: Loan shark's office · Chop shop · Embassy courier · Bank of Solace (front) · Night club safe ·
  Port authority · **Hale's informant safe-house (boss)**: you steal *her* files.
- Story: the files show Echo's payments go back ten years and are still being paid today.
- **Cliffhanger:** the last payment was sent from Ash's account.

### 7. Ash (8 jobs)
*Ash is back. He wants the crew. He wants you.*
- New: **the outside man.** Some chapters there may be *no* snitch at the table: the tip-offs come from
  outside, and every accusation is wrong. Knowing when *not* to accuse is the test.
- Jobs: Ash's first job with you · Old haunts · Hale's car · The church collection · Rival crew's stash ·
  The tunnel · Ash's last debt · **The Harbour, again (boss)**
- Story: Ash says *you* sold him. You say *he* did. Only the evidence files can settle it.
- **Cliffhanger:** Hale arrests Ash. Ash, in cuffs: *"Ask her who Echo really works for."*

### 8. Solace (finale: one heist in 8 parts)
*The Port Solace Mint. Every job is one stage of the same heist, and everything carries over.*
- Stages: Get the uniforms · The guard rota · The drain plans · Bribe the inspector · Kill the cameras ·
  The tunnel · The vault · **The way out**
- Heat, the crew, who's lifted and the cash all carry from stage to stage. One snitch can sink the whole heist at the last stage.

## The endings (picked by what you found and what you choose)
| Ending | How | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Solace** | Finish the Mint | You take the money and leave Port Solace. Echo is never named. |
| **Ash** | Finish the Mint and hand Ash to Hale | You walk free. Hale gets the wrong person. |
| **Echo (true ending)** | All 24 evidence files + finish the Mint | The truth: **Echo was Juno all along.** She sold Ash ten years ago and has played you, Ash and Hale against each other ever since. Your final choice: turn her in, or bring her in. |

The true ending is earned by playing well (3 stars), not by luck. That makes replaying worth it.

## Difficulty (not easy, by design)
- **Modes:** Street (learn), Made (real game), Kingpin (brutal: one tail, bad luck 1 in 4).
- **What makes it hard:** fewer clues in later chapters, two snitches, a snitch who recruits, loyalty,
  heat that carries, crew who get lifted, rush jobs on a timer.
- **Stars:** 1 = got out with cash · 2 = took 40% of the chapter · 3 = took everything (in practice
  only possible by catching the snitch). Next chapter needs 1 star. Evidence files need 3 stars on a job.
- **Fail:** raid (heat 3) means the chapter is lost and you replay it with a fresh secret snitch.

## What keeps people coming back
- A **cliffhanger** at every chapter end, plus Echo's texts after every job.
- **Evidence board** in the hideout fills up as you play (24 files), showing how close you are to the truth.
- **Crew grows:** members gain skill with jobs survived; losing a good one hurts.
- **Daily job:** one shared job a day, the same secret draw for everyone, with a leaderboard.
- **Kingpin mode** and 3-star replays for players who want the hardest version.

## What happens on-chain (why it needs encryption)
| Part of the game | What stays secret | Why it would break if it were public |
|---|---|---|
| Who the snitch is (and the skimmer, the second snitch, the recruit) | Drawn with on-chain randomness, encrypted until the run ends | Anyone reading the chain would know who to accuse |
| Whether the police were tipped | Worked out in secret from the team you sent and the snitch | The suspense, and the clue it gives you, would be gone |
| Tails, rumours, the wiretap | Each answer is encrypted and only you can read it | Other players could copy your clues; the snitch could see them too |
| The bag and heat | Encrypted totals | Rivals and friends couldn't bluff about how well they're doing |
| In-job choices (scout, roles, decisions) | Public settings that change the odds | Kept public on purpose, so anyone can check the rules are fair |
At the end, the proof that comes with the reveal lets anyone check that the snitch was fixed from the start and not changed afterwards.
