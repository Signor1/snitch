## Notes

- **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com) models, textures and HDRIs are CC0. Files were decimated, re-pivoted, resized and re-encoded (WebP) for the web.
- **Gazebo Fuel / Open Robotics** models (https://app.gazebosim.org/OpenRobotics/fuel) are listed as "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal" by the Fuel API at download time.
- **MakeHuman / MPFB2**: characters were generated with MPFB 2.0.18 (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html). MakeHuman output and the `_cc0` asset packs used (makehuman_system_assets, shirts01, pants01, shoes01, suits01, gloves01, eyebrows01) are CC0. Clothing from the CC BY packs is listed in its own section below.
- **Original procedural models** (safe, pallet, amplifier, OPEN sign, security grille, shop door, display counter, police cap/belt/badge) were generated by scripts in `assets-src/scripts/` for this project and are released CC0.
- The skeleton inside `real/characters/*.glb` reproduces the bone layout of the Quaternius Universal Animation Library rig (CC0) already used by the game.
